## Smoke test (explicitly NOT the acceptance ablation)

`reports/improvement/16/01_smoke_harness.py` fit a price-free conditional
logit (step 10's own frozen `l2`, no re-tuning) on dev_core **with the single
archived day excluded**, then scored the 21 held-out archived-day races —
proving the harness executes end-to-end, not a chronological forecast claim.
Result: `no_text`/`regex_text`/`hosted_text` mean race log loss identical to
~1e-12 (paired-bootstrap CI95 `[-8.7e-12, +6.2e-12]` for both regex and
hosted vs no-text). This is the *expected, structural* consequence of having
only one archived day: with that day excluded from training, every text
column is 100% missing for all 263,755 training rows, so `ConditionalLogitModel`'s
`nanmean`/`nanstd` standardisation degrades that column to a constant
zero-contribution feature — the fitted weight cannot be anything but ~0
regardless of whether text "would" help. This is evidence for *why* the stage
is data-deferred, not evidence text is useless. Full output:
`reports/improvement/16/smoke_scorecard.json`.
